The Journey

From Vision to Masterpiece

Five steps from first idea to the table. Most custom cakes need two to three weeks.

1

Inspiration

We begin with your ideas, moodboards, and dreams.

2

Flavor Profile

We talk flavours and taste samples, then agree servings, budget and date.

3

Design Sketch

Our designer sketches your cake and shares a finished concept for approval.

4

Artisan Baking

Your cake is baked and decorated by hand in the week of your event.

5

Delivery

We deliver, set up and style the cake at your venue.
